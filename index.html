01 — About Me

Pattern recognition across disciplines.

My edge is seeing structural failures where others see isolated incidents. I've diagnosed a billion-rupee counterfeiting gap in India's packaged-water supply chain, built real-time digital twins of a 500 MW coal unit and Italy's first net-zero micro-grid, shipped production web apps used on live construction sites, and managed cross-functional operations at one of India's largest power utilities — all before my final year of college.

I think in frameworks, build in weeks, and communicate in outcomes. Engineering gave me the discipline to stress-test ideas against physical reality; I bring that same rigour to energy systems, operations, and product problems.

Recently completed an engineering internship at NTPC Singrauli (2,000 MW), from Singrauli, MP — studying Mechanical Engineering at Amity University, Noida (CGPA 8.84). Selected for the Air India Cadet Pilot Program.
